Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Built for Balance is a free app that helps students notice and manage burnout. It is run by Built for Balance Foundation, a nonprofit organization ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Who the app is for

The app is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at the address below and we will delete the account and its data.

Information we collect

  • Account details. Your name and email address, and how you sign in (Google, Apple, or email and password). If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we receive the private relay address Apple provides. We never see your Google or Apple password.
  • Profile information. Your age, school level, and optional profile details such as a bio or photo, plus the preferences you choose during setup (for example your situation, responsibilities, and priorities).
  • Wellness information. Your answers to surveys and quizzes, the scores and feedback calculated from them, your goals and progress, and any journal or feedback entries you write.
  • Community content. Forum posts you publish, friend connections, and chat messages you send to friends.
  • App activity. Points, levels, streaks, badges, and cosmetic items you earn or choose.
  • Safety records. Reports you file about other users' content, and the list of users you have blocked.
  • AI chat messages. Text you send to the in-app assistant, Bobby (see "AI assistant" below).

How we use information

  • To create your account, keep you signed in, and run the features you use.
  • To calculate and show your burnout and wellness results, goals, and progress.
  • To power the community features: the forum wall, friends, chat, and leaderboard.
  • To keep the community safe by reviewing reports and removing content or accounts that break our community rules.

AI assistant

Bobby is powered by Google's Gemini service. When you send Bobby a message, the text you typed and the earlier messages in that conversation are sent to Google to generate a reply. We do not send your name, email, survey answers, or profile to the assistant. You are asked to agree before your first message is sent. Please do not share private details such as your full name, address, or passwords in the chat.

Bobby can make mistakes and is not a doctor, counselor, or crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services, or call or text 988 in the US.

Who can see your information

  • Other users. Your forum posts, with your name, can be seen by other signed-in users. Your name, level, and badges are visible to people who search for you or are your friends. Chat messages are visible to you and the friend you are chatting with.
  • Our service providers. We use Google's Firebase platform to sign you in and store the app's data on Google Cloud servers in the United States. We use Google's Gemini service for the AI assistant. Apple and Google also process your sign-in when you choose those options.
  • Program leaders. A small number of Built for Balance administrators can review reports and manage accounts for safety and support.
  • The law. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Community safety

You can report any post or message and block any user from the menu on that content. Blocked users' content is hidden from you and they cannot message you. Reports are reviewed by the Built for Balance team. We may keep reports for safety and record-keeping purposes even after the account that filed them is deleted.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings, then Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your profile, survey answers, goals, journal and feedback entries, forum posts, the messages you sent, your friend connections, your block list, and your sign-in. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the connection to the app. Messages that other people sent to you in a shared chat, and safety reports, may remain after your account is deleted.

If you cannot use the app to delete your account, email us and we will do it for you.

Keeping information safe

Data is stored with Google's Firebase, which encrypts data in transit, and access to the database is controlled by security rules that limit who can read and change each kind of data. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong password and keep your device locked.

Your choices

  • Update your profile and preferences in the app.
  • Choose whether to use the AI assistant; the app asks first.
  • Block and report other users.
  • Delete your account, or ask us to access or correct your information, by emailing us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell people in the app.
