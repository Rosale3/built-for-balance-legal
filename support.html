Built for Balance  /  Privacy Policy

Support

Questions, problems, or feedback about the Built for Balance app? Email builtforbalance21@gmail.com. Please include the email you signed up with and what you were doing when the problem happened. We read every message.

Built for Balance is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services, or call or text 988 in the US. The in-app assistant, Bobby, is not a substitute for a doctor or counselor.

Delete your account

Open the app, go to Settings, and choose Delete Account. This removes your profile, survey answers, goals, posts, messages you sent, and your sign-in. If you cannot use the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account for you.

Report a post or message, or block someone

Tap the ⋮ button next to a forum post or chat message, or beside a friend's name in a chat. Choose Report to tell us what is wrong, or Block to hide that person's content and stop them messaging you. To undo a block, open "Blocked users" at the top of the Social page and tap Unblock. We review reports and remove content or accounts that break our community rules.

Sign-in problems

  • Make sure you use the same sign-in method you used when you created your account (Google, Apple, or email and password).
  • Update the app and your iPhone to the latest version.
  • If it still fails, email us with the sign-in method you chose and any message you saw.
